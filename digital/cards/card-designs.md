## WHATSAPP CATALOG CARDS

For each menu item in WhatsApp Business catalog:

### Format:
- **Photo**: Square (1:1), product on wooden surface, warm lighting
- **Title**: Item name in Spanish
- **Description**: 1-2 lines + price
- **Example**:
  ```
  Photo: [Stroopwafel on wooden board with coffee]
  Title: Stroopwafel Clásico
  Description: Fresco, crujiente, con relleno de caramelo y canela. 
  Hecho al momento. Gs. 15.000
  ```
