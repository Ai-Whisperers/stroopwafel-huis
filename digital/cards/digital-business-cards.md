## DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD (vCard)

### For each team member, create:
- Name
- Title: "Co-Founder, Stroopwafel Huis"
- Phone (Paraguay mobile)
- Email: name@stroopwafelhuis.com
- Website: stroopwafelhuis.com
- Instagram: @stroopwafelhuis
- Location: Asuncion, Paraguay
- Logo attached

### Format: 
- **Apple Wallet / Google Wallet** compatible
- **QR code** that opens vCard for saving
- **NFC tag** option for physical card tap-to-share

### Tools to create:
- **Popl** (popl.co) — NFC + digital card, ~$8/card
- **Blinq** (blinq.me) — free digital cards with QR
- **HiHello** (hihello.me) — free, professional
- **Canva** — design visual card for WhatsApp/social sharing
