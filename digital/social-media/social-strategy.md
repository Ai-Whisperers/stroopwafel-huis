# Social Media Strategy

---

## PLATFORMS (Priority Order)

### 1. Instagram (PRIMARY) — @stroopwafelhuis
- **Why**: #1 platform for food in Paraguay. Visual = perfect for stroopwafels.
- **Goal**: 5,000 followers in 6 months
- **Content mix**: 40% product photos, 30% behind-the-scenes/making, 20% Dutch culture, 10% reposts/user content
- **Post frequency**: 5x/week (feed), daily stories
- **Reels**: 3x/week minimum (stroopwafel making is ASMR gold)

### 2. TikTok — @stroopwafelhuis
- **Why**: Stroopwafel-making videos are inherently viral (satisfying, ASMR, food porn)
- **Goal**: 10,000 followers in 6 months (easier to grow on TikTok)
- **Content**: All video — pressing, splitting, filling, topping, eating
- **Post frequency**: 4-5x/week

### 3. WhatsApp Business
- **Why**: Paraguay's #1 messaging app. Direct orders, customer service.
- **Catalog**: Full menu with photos
- **Broadcast lists**: Weekly specials, new flavors

### 4. Facebook — Stroopwafel Huis
- **Why**: Still important in Paraguay, especially for 30+ age group and events
- **Content**: Share Instagram posts, events, promotions
- **Facebook Events**: King's Day party, grand opening, workshops

### 5. Google My Business
- **Why**: LOCAL search is how people find cafes
- **Critical**: Must be set up BEFORE opening with photos, hours, menu

## INFLUENCER STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (2 months before)
- Send gift boxes to 20 Asuncion food influencers
- Include handwritten note + story of the concept
- No posting requirement — genuine interest is better

### Phase 2: Opening Week
- Invite top 10 food bloggers for exclusive preview night
- Professional photos of them with stroopwafels
- Encourage honest reviews

### Phase 3: Ongoing
- Monthly "Influencer Date" — 2 influencers invited for free tasting + content creation
- Micro-influencer program: anyone with 1K+ followers gets 15% off with code
- Track engagement: which influencers drive actual customers?
