### WHERE TO BUY STROOPWAFEL IRONS:
- **Krampouz** (France) — Professional crepe/wafel irons
- **Eurogast/Saro** (Netherlands) — Dutch catering equipment
- **AliExpress/Alibaba** — Chinese-made stroopwafel irons, $200-500 each (quality varies!)
- **Custom from Netherlands** — Contact Dutch bakery equipment suppliers
- **eBay Netherlands** — Second-hand commercial irons
- Recommended: Order 2 irons from a Dutch supplier, 1 backup from Alibaba
